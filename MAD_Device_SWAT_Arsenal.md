# MAD Device SWAT Arsenal Checklist
## Expanded for Hardware / Product Evaluation (to be used by AA, DA, and especially SSA)

This checklist is now part of the living knowledge base. DA should systematically pressure-test these items. SSA must include residual uncertainties and next-best alternatives on these dimensions.

### Durability & Environmental
- IP rating / waterproof / water resistance (glasses, ring, case separately)
- Sweat, rain, splashes, submersion limits
- Temperature extremes, dust
- Drop / impact resistance, materials durability
- Cleaning and maintenance requirements

### Navigation & Connectivity
- GPS source (phone GPS vs onboard)
- Map provider (Google Maps, proprietary, other)
- Offline capability (maps, translation, AI features)
- Simultaneous use of modes (e.g., navigation + translation/Conversate)
- Driving / walking / cycling support and safety restrictions
- Accuracy and reliability vs native Google Maps

### Power & Charging
- Battery life (typical vs continuous heavy use)
- Charging method (case only, direct cable, wireless)
- Case capacity and charging cable included
- Charge time and indicators
- Battery degradation notes

### Security & Ownership
- Pairing / account binding model
- Can a stolen device be easily paired to a new phone/account?
- Factory reset / remove from account process
- Data remaining on device after reset
- Account recovery / remote wipe options (if any)
- Privacy of microphones, data storage location

### Cost, Value & Alternatives
- Total cost of ownership (device + required accessories + prescriptions + subscriptions)
- Next-best alternatives at similar or lower price (value, durability, feature completeness)
- Durability vs price trade-offs
- Ecosystem lock-in risks
- Resale / repairability

### Controls & Usability
- Primary control method (ring, touch, voice)
- Friction of mode switching
- Accessibility for specific workflows (e.g., language buffering without phone glances)

### Horizon & Upcoming Alternatives ("Is it worth waiting?")
- Known or rumored next-generation devices from major players (Meta, Apple, Google/Samsung, Snap, Xreal, Alibaba, etc.)
- Expected release windows, confirmed vs rumored status
- Key feature leaps (better displays, offline capability, true simultaneous modes, lower price, better battery, stronger AI)
- Specific relevance to the current use case (e.g., selective language buffering, glanceable lessons, privacy-first)
- Opportunity cost of waiting vs buying now (current product maturity vs expected improvements)
- Broader applicability: same logic for apps, software platforms, and services (upcoming model releases, competing apps, platform shifts)

### Failure Modes to Always Flag
- Internet / phone dependence
- Mode exclusivity (cannot run X and Y together)
- Early-adopter software instability
- Hidden costs or required accessories for full function
- Security/ownership transfer friction or ease
- Risk of rapid obsolescence if a superior alternative is imminent

---

**SSA instruction**: When synthesizing a device (or app/software) evaluation, explicitly score or comment on each relevant category above, list residual uncertainties, and **always recommend 1–2 next-best alternatives** (with rationale on value, price, durability, or fit) as a standard closing element. 

- For a single-item case study: recommend the strongest alternative(s) even if not explicitly requested.
- For a two-item comparison: compare the two, then still recommend a further next-best alternative if one exists.
- Always address the Horizon question: is there a credible reason to wait, and for how long?
