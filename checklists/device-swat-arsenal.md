# MAD Device SWAT Arsenal Checklist

Expanded for hardware / product evaluation.  
DA should systematically pressure-test these items.  
SSA must include residual uncertainties and next-best alternatives.

## Durability & Environmental
- IP rating / waterproof / water resistance (glasses, ring, case separately)
- Sweat, rain, splashes, submersion limits
- Temperature extremes, dust
- Drop / impact resistance, materials durability
- Cleaning and maintenance requirements

## Navigation & Connectivity
- GPS source (phone GPS vs onboard)
- Map provider (Google Maps, proprietary, other)
- Offline capability (maps, translation, AI features)
- Simultaneous use of modes (e.g., navigation + translation)
- Driving / walking / cycling support and safety restrictions
- Accuracy and reliability vs native maps

## Power & Charging
- Battery life (typical vs continuous heavy use)
- Charging method (case only, direct cable, wireless)
- Case capacity and charging cable included
- Charge time and indicators
- Battery degradation notes

## Security & Ownership
- Pairing / account binding model
- Can a stolen device be easily paired to a new phone/account?
- Factory reset / remove from account process
- Data remaining on device after reset
- Account recovery / remote wipe options (if any)
- Privacy of microphones, data storage location

## Cost, Value & Alternatives
- Total cost of ownership
- Next-best alternatives at similar or lower price
- Durability vs price trade-offs
- Ecosystem lock-in risks
- Resale / repairability

## Source Quality & Recency
- Are key specs confirmed in recent (< 6 months) independent reviews?
- Are battery/AI/connectivity claims corroborated across multiple non-manufacturer sources?
- Has the product received significant firmware or software updates since the most-cited reviews?

## Horizon & Upcoming Alternatives
- Known or rumored next-generation devices
- Expected release windows, confirmed vs rumored
- Key feature leaps
- Opportunity cost of waiting vs buying now

## Failure Modes to Always Flag
- Internet / phone dependence
- Mode exclusivity
- Early-adopter software instability
- Hidden costs or required accessories
- Security/ownership transfer friction
- Risk of rapid obsolescence

---
**SSA instruction**: Always recommend 1–2 next-best alternatives and address whether it is worth waiting.
