# Topic Type: Price Ferret

**Use for:** Finding the best price for a specific product or service, including timing, hidden costs, and platform comparisons.

## AA Framing
Find and compile the best available prices across all relevant platforms, retailers, and regions. Include current deals, cashback, bundle offers, and price history where available. Be thorough — surface options the user may not have thought to check.

## DA Pressure Angles
- Source recency: are prices live or cached? When was each checked?
- Hidden costs: shipping, import duties, GST, installation, required accessories
- Platform trust: is the seller reputable? Return policy? Warranty validity?
- Price history: is this actually a good price, or is it always this price?
- Alternative purchasing paths: grey market, refurbished, open-box, rental
- Subscription vs one-time cost trap
- Are there known upcoming sales events that make waiting worthwhile?
- Currency and regional pricing discrepancies

## SSA Requirements
- Present a ranked price comparison with total cost of ownership
- Flag any prices that could not be independently verified
- Address the timing question explicitly: buy now or wait?
- Note residual uncertainty on any price that may have changed
