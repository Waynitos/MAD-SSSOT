# Topic Type: Research

**Use for:** Investigating a factual, technical, or analytical question where completeness, source quality, and currency matter.

## AA Framing
Build the most complete, well-sourced answer possible. Cover the mainstream understanding, key evidence, leading experts or organisations, and practical implications. Prefer primary sources (papers, official docs, data) over secondary summaries. Note where evidence is strong vs emerging.

## DA Pressure Angles
- Source Quality Audit (mandatory):
  - Publication date of each key claim — flag anything older than 6 months for fast-moving topics
  - Primary vs secondary source — flag secondary-only claims
  - Single-outlet claims — flag for corroboration
  - Potential AI-generated sources with no traceable primary
- Counter-evidence: what do critics or dissenting researchers say?
- What the mainstream view misses or underweights
- Jurisdictional or contextual limits (does this apply to Australia? to this industry?)
- Recency: has anything changed in the last 6–12 months that affects the answer?
- Are there known upcoming publications, decisions, or events that would change the answer?

## SSA Requirements
- Separate well-established from emerging/contested findings
- Flag any claims that rest on a single source
- Include a "what we don't know yet" section
- Recommend 2–3 further reading sources the user can verify independently
