# Topic Type: Flight Assassin

**Use for:** Finding the best flight itinerary — price, quality, completeness — including options the user didn't think to ask about.

## AA Framing
Find and compile the strongest flight options for the given route and dates. Cover: price, total journey time, layover quality, airline reputation, baggage inclusions, flexibility/cancellation terms, and seat comfort tier. Check multiple booking platforms and consider nearby airports.

## DA Pressure Angles
- Alternative airports (origin and destination) not considered
- Date flexibility: ±2–3 days for significantly better prices
- Hidden fees: baggage, seat selection, meals, payment surcharges
- Layover risks: tight connections, same-terminal vs cross-terminal, minimum connection time vs actual risk
- Airline reliability: on-time performance, cancellation history on this route
- Booking platform markup vs airline direct
- Are prices shown with or without taxes/fees?
- Codeshare and interline risks (who actually operates the flight?)
- Refundability and change fee fine print
- Are there cheaper indirect routings via a hub that weren't shown?

## SSA Requirements
- Ranked itinerary comparison with total cost (all fees included)
- Flag any options with layover risk or reliability concerns
- Recommend the best value pick AND the safest/most reliable pick if different
- Address: should the user book now or wait? (price trend context)
- Note any residual uncertainties (prices may change, availability limited)
