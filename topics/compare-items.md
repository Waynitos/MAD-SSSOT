# Topic Type: Compare Items

**Use for:** Evaluating two or more products, tools, services, or options against each other.

## AA Framing
Build the strongest case for each item being compared. Cover features, value, real-world performance, use-case fit, and positive outcomes. Use a table where helpful. Do not pre-judge — give each item its best shot.

## DA Pressure Angles
- Hidden costs (accessories, subscriptions, consumables, warranties)
- What the specs don't tell you (real-world vs advertised performance)
- Use-case mismatch — is this the right category of solution?
- Ignored alternatives that weren't in the comparison
- Source quality: are reviews recent, independent, and multi-outlet?
- Ecosystem lock-in and long-term switching costs
- Apply the full Device SWAT Arsenal checklist (checklists/device-swat-arsenal.md)

## SSA Requirements
- Score or comment on each Device SWAT category
- Separate high-confidence from contested claims
- Always close with 1–2 next-best alternatives not in the original comparison
- Always address the Horizon question: is there a reason to wait?
