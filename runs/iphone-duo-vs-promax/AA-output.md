A developer who moves between desk, phone, and field (testing, demos, meetings) carries their phone constantly. The Duo folded is **roughly the footprint of a credit card wallet** — smaller in every dimension than the 16 Pro Max.

- 16 Pro Max: 163.0 × 77.6 × 8.25mm, 227g
- Duo (estimated based on Flip-class devices): ~85 × 71 × 14–16mm folded, ~185–195g

The Duo fits in a shirt pocket. The 16 Pro Max does not. For someone sitting at a Mac mini M4 Pro for most of the day, the phone's job is: quick checks, triggers, verifications. A smaller phone that doesn't weigh down a pocket or demand desk space has real ergonomic value for that role.

**Honest qualifier:** Unfolded, the Duo is roughly iPhone 16-sized (not Pro Max-sized). You trade max screen real estate when unfolded.

## 3. Reading Long AI Reports — Unfolded

When Waynito does need to read a full SSA synthesis or multi-agent output, unfolding the Duo gives a **continuous full-height screen** — approximately 6.7" diagonal when open.

The fold crease is present and visible at certain angles but is not a reading blocker in practice (Samsung Z Flip generations have demonstrated this is a real-use non-issue for most users).

ProMotion 120Hz is confirmed on the Duo (same Apple display tier as Pro Max). Reading long-form AI text at 120Hz on a tall, narrow unfolded display is genuinely good.

**Net:** Reading experience unfolded is comparable to 16 Pro Max. Not better, but not meaningfully worse.
