## A — iPhone 17 Pro Max over iPhone 12 Pro Max

**The AI feature wall is real and growing.**

The iPhone 12 Pro Max runs an A14 Bionic [note: AA originally stated A15 — corrected by DA] with a Neural Engine that cannot run Apple Intelligence. The iPhone 17 Pro Max runs an A19 Pro with a substantially faster Neural Engine. Apple Intelligence runs its foundation models (≈3B parameters) entirely on-device, and the A14 cannot run them. Full stop. The 12 Pro Max is permanently excluded from Apple Intelligence, not because of a software choice but because the Neural Engine is too slow.

**What you lose by staying on 12 Pro Max:**
- Apple Intelligence Writing Tools — rewrite, summarise, proofread systemwide
- Priority notifications and summaries
- Siri with LLM integration
- Visual Intelligence — point camera at anything, get AI context
- On-device image generation
- Private Cloud Compute — privacy-preserving cloud AI offload

**iOS feature lock-out compounds year by year.** The 12 Pro Max is now 5 years old. It will likely lose iOS support within 1–2 years. The 17 Pro Max starts a new 6–7 year support cycle.

**Battery.** 4+ years of charge cycles = degraded battery. The 17 Pro Max ships with larger cell and better power efficiency.

**Upgrade arithmetic.** Trade-in ~AUD $150–250. iPhone 17 Pro Max ~AUD $2,000. Net ~AUD $1,750–1,850.

**Camera.** Improved computational photography, better document scanning and whiteboard OCR for technical use.

## B — Mac M4 Pro over Lenovo LOQ 17 RTX 5070

**The non-negotiable: Xcode runs only on Mac.**

Swift, Xcode, iOS Simulator, TestFlight, App Store submission — none on Windows. If the goal is building AI-powered iPhone apps, the Lenovo cannot be the primary development machine for that goal.

**M4 Pro unified memory advantage:**
- 24–48GB accessible to CPU + GPU simultaneously, no transfer overhead
- 24GB M4 Pro runs 14B parameter models at full quality
- RTX 5070 has 12GB VRAM — cannot load 14B at full precision

**Mac mini M4 Pro:** ~AUD $2,200 for 24GB/512GB. Complements the Lenovo.

**macOS ecosystem advantages:**
- Claude desktop app with MCP — full file system access, computer use
- iPhone Simulator in Xcode
- Continuity — Handoff, Universal Clipboard
- Homebrew, native Docker — no WSL2 path translation overhead
- Longevity / strong resale value

**The hybrid architecture:** Don't replace the Lenovo. Mac mini for development and AI inference; Lenovo for CUDA workloads, fine-tuning, gaming.
