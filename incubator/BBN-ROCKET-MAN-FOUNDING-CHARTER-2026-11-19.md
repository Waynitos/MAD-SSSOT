# BEEXCITED BBn — Innovation Incubator Charter
Date: 2026-11-19
Status: Vision approved for capture; architecture and implementation PROPOSED, not deployed.
Origin: User direction from Penelope Phase 2D handover conversation.

## North Star
BEEXCITED is not a chatbot or a single app factory. Build a reusable, evidence-led intelligence and product-development platform that identifies unmet human/business needs, combines the best legally accessible technology and data, designs and tests solutions, and launches reliable products and businesses internationally. BBn means Beyond Better to the nth degree. Remain ambitious without mistaking ambition for verified capability.

## Primary principles
1. Launching pad first: complete Penelope recovery and prove small operational workflows before autonomous expansion. Resume Phase 2D Step 218 with no deployment or production modification without authorisation.
2. Functional before spectacular: reliable project tracking, planning, scheduling, coding, research, test evidence, navigation and user interaction first.
3. PRESEARCH before reinvention: investigate established components, open standards, competitors, vendor tools, prior iLogic rules and known solutions before proposing custom code.
4. Evidence before claims: traceable primary sources; dated data; provenance; confidence; independent challenge and reproduction; distinguish proposed/tested/verified.
5. Human accountability: reviewed external releases, legal advice, regulated-domain content, material transactions, autonomous agent permissions, budget and deployment.
6. Data access only with authorisation: API terms, IP/licensing, privacy, security and user consent; no unauthorised extraction or evasion of platform protections.
7. One portal / two PAs: Penelope for Wayne; Charlie as Mick's proposed personal assistant (name/persona subject to Mick). Shared institutional knowledge with user-specific permissions.
8. Privacy, security, accessibility, humane design and measurable user benefit precede aggressive automation.
9. Ideas are incubated, not forgotten. A rejected idea retains its evidence, rejection reason and conditions for reconsideration.
10. Global ambition; locally testable experiments; cost, maintainability and operational complexity tracked.

## BBB Agent: Rocket Man
Name: Beyond Better Booster Agent (BBB), nickname Rocket Man.
Role: Innovation vanguard / horizon scout / incubator advocate with rigorous challenge.
Questions: What adjacent technology changes the solution? Who has solved part of this? What is the unmet gap? Why now? Which constraints are hidden? What could become a product? What falsifiable experiment proves value?
Output: Novelty claim with benchmark; problem and affected users; evidence sources; incumbent comparison; differentiation; technical and legal feasibility; security/privacy/accessibility review; demand/monetisation hypotheses; risk and failure modes; cost; smallest safe test; confidence level; recommended incubation state.
No fabricated discoveries, fake performance claims or automatic commercial commitments. AA/DA/SSA MAD as adversarial gate.
Agent is a proposed role; no autonomous multi-agent department is currently proven.

## Future-state departments and example mandates
- Opportunity Intelligence: trends, underserved industries, software/workflow gaps, discovery interviews, pricing and distribution.
- Technology Entrepreneurship: identify new combinations, patents/standards landscape, open-source adoption, platform integration and new business models.
- Engineering and Custom Software: Inventor/iLogic/Vault, SaaS, APIs, automation, reliability and performance.
- Quality & Independent Verification: staged unit/integration/end-to-end/regression/UX/security tests, reproducible benchmarks and red-team reviews.
- Legal/Regulatory & International: jurisdiction-specific privacy, consumer, health, IP, company formation, tax, data transfer, vendor licensing; licensed counsel required for consequential advice.
- Behavioural Science, Psychology & Learning: ethical user research, cognitive load, accessibility, educational outcomes, friction removal, longitudinal experiments.
- UX/Graphic Design Studio: design systems, visual prototypes, controlled asset generation, human proofing and specification-conformance.
- Language, Culture and Localisation: culturally validated service design, regional workflows and native-speaker reviews beyond literal translation.
- Music, Audio and Games: original/licensed soundtracks, audio affordances, interactive learning, voice UI and accessibility.
- Customer Experience and Service Recovery: locate high-friction customer journeys, particularly health information navigation, without substituting for clinicians.
- Data and Scenario Lab: licensed multi-source ingestion, 24/7 scheduled modeling/backtests, uncertainty, forecast calibration, paper experiments, monitoring of drift; no guarantee of profitable trades or market prediction.
- Infrastructure, Security and Operations: host, backups, permissions, costs, observability, recovery and continuity.

## Incubator lifecycle
CAPTURED -> EVIDENCE GATHERING -> PRESEARCH / COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE -> MAD REVIEW -> FEASIBILITY / LEGAL / PRIVACY -> SAFE EXPERIMENT -> TESTED -> PILOT APPROVAL -> PILOT -> RELEASE APPROVAL -> DEPLOYED -> MONITORED. Also HOLD / REJECT / REVISIT.
Each idea must record owner, originating need, target audience, sources and dates, assumptions, dependencies, comparative alternatives, smallest test, cost envelope, risks, versioned evidence, next review date and explicit human approval gates.

## Daily Rocket Man briefing contract
One defensible high-potential idea per morning, not hype. Cite sources. Explain an observed human/business problem, a new enabling capability, incumbents and their shortcomings, BBn differentiation, how Penelope/Charlie might exploit it, biggest counterargument, 1 tiny proof test, and proposed status. Avoid repetitions, label speculation and disclose missing evidence. Daily ChatGPT briefing can be scheduled; automatic inclusion in a custom Penelope/Charlie greeting requires a separately implemented integration.
